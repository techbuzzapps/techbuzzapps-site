How This Path Works

Intuition first. Every idea starts with something you can picture — apples, coins, steps along a line, slices of pizza — before it gets a name or a rule. You should understand why something works before being asked to remember it.

Each lesson follows the same rhythm: an explanation, a visual, worked examples, and practice questions to try on your own. The levels build on each other, so if you're starting from scratch — or it has simply been a long time — begin at Level 1.
