## Verified product and commerce facts

- App display name Color Blitz; bundle identifier `com.aniadhikary.ColorBlitz`. **VERIFIED IN CURRENT SOURCE**.
- Two StoreKit 2 auto-renewing subscription products grant one shared Premium entitlement. Premium suppresses forced interstitials and unlocks Color Master; optional rewarded ads remain. **VERIFIED IN CURRENT SOURCE**.
- ColorBlitz stores gameplay/progression locally and submits eligible high scores through Apple Game Center. **VERIFIED IN CURRENT SOURCE**.
- An eligible competitive run is associated with the Game Center account signed in when that run starts. Signed-out runs and historical sessions with unknown ownership remain local-only and are not automatically assigned to a later account. Local session history and progression remain separate from Game Center account changes. **VERIFIED IN CURRENT SOURCE**. Any terms describing this behavior require **LEGAL APPROVAL REQUIRED**.

## Proposed Terms sections

1. Operator identity, support contact, effective date, and territory/jurisdiction.
2. Eligibility and permitted use of the app; intellectual property and brand assets.
3. Local game progress, device backup limitations, availability, updates, and compatibility.
4. Game Center account and leaderboard rules, score eligibility, abuse handling.
5. In-app coins, non-transferability/expiration treatment, rewarded ads, and game-mode access; avoid terms not confirmed by product owner.
6. Premium subscription products, localized price at confirmation, billing/renewal/cancellation/refund handling through Apple, and material feature description.
7. Third-party services, Apple platform terms, advertising and consent links.
8. Privacy Policy link and data practices, aligned with the separate approved policy.
9. Disclaimers, limitation of liability, termination, changes, and contact process.
10. Governing law, dispute mechanisms, consumer rights, and any jurisdiction-specific notices.

All proposed terms require **LEGAL APPROVAL REQUIRED**. Do not invent legal obligations, governing law, dispute process, or consumer-rights waivers.
