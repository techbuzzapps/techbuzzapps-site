# ColorBlitz Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

ColorBlitz is a color-matching and brain-training game developed by TechBuzzApps. This draft explains how information is stored and handled when you play ColorBlitz. It is not ready for publication until the review items in the [verification report](ColorBlitz_Privacy_Policy_Verification_Report.md) are resolved and the policy is approved.

### 1. Game Data and Progress

ColorBlitz stores game-related information locally on your device, including:

- Game sessions, scores, history, and personal bests
- Coins, XP, reward records, and game progress
- Daily Challenge completion information
- Game preferences and settings
- A local player identifier used by the app

The app uses this information to support gameplay and progression.

### 2. Game Center

ColorBlitz integrates with Apple Game Center for supported leaderboards. When a competitive run begins while Game Center is signed in, ColorBlitz stores the associated Game Center account identifier with that newly completed run. This lets the app associate eligible scores with the account used for the run.

Runs completed while signed out, and older runs whose account ownership is unknown, remain local and are not automatically assigned to another account after sign-in or an account switch. For eligible scores, the app can submit scores to Game Center and request leaderboard information. Apple processes Game Center information under its own privacy practices.

### 3. Advertising

ColorBlitz integrates Google Mobile Ads to provide interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present applicable consent choices. The app requests ads only when UMP indicates that ad requests may proceed; if consent information cannot be refreshed, the app disables ad requests. Available privacy choices depend on the applicable consent flow and region.

Google's documentation and the reviewed Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy manifest describe categories that may include device identifiers, IP address or approximate location, advertising interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, crash information, and performance information. The SDK manifest also declares Device ID as tracking data. The presence of an SDK or a category in its manifest does not establish that ColorBlitz transmits every category in every situation. The data applicable to the released app, its ad configuration, consent choices, and Apple's tracking requirements must be confirmed before publication.

Google may use information it processes under its own privacy practices and the applicable advertising settings. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). **Review before publication:** Confirm the release SDK disclosures, ad and mediation configuration, consent behavior, whether tracking occurs, and any required App Tracking Transparency treatment. Do not imply that the app does or does not track until this review is complete.

### 4. Premium Subscriptions

ColorBlitz offers optional monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Apple handles subscription purchases and billing. ColorBlitz uses purchase and entitlement information provided through StoreKit to determine access to Premium features. The app does not store Premium entitlement state in its local game database.

Premium removes forced interstitial advertisements but does not remove optional rewarded advertisements. Apple's handling of purchase and subscription information is described in Apple's own privacy materials.

### 5. Notifications

ColorBlitz may offer an optional Daily Challenge reminder. If you enable it and grant notification permission, the app schedules a local reminder on your device. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings and turn the reminder off in the app.

### 6. Third-Party Services

ColorBlitz integrates with Apple Game Center, Apple StoreKit, Google Mobile Ads, and Google UMP for the features described above. These services may process information according to their own privacy practices. Their exact processing, retention, and configuration for the released app must be confirmed; SDK integration alone does not establish which information is transmitted in a particular use.

The reviewed app source did not show an app-owned gameplay backend or a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. This source review does not determine all network activity of third-party SDKs. **Review before publication:** Confirm release-build network destinations and the final SDK inventory.

### 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally in the app's database and device preferences. The reviewed app does not provide an in-app tool to export, reset, or delete all game data, and the app does not define a retention period. The database is not explicitly excluded from device backup. Backup, restore, and app-removal behavior has not been verified, so this policy does not promise that uninstalling the app deletes every copy of locally stored information.

Apple and Google handle information processed through their services under their respective practices. For questions or requests, contact us at **techbuzzapps@gmail.com**. **Review before publication:** Confirm what requests can be fulfilled, how local data can be removed, and applicable backup and provider-side deletion behavior.
