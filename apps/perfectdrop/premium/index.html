Perfect Drop Premium

Unlock the challenge modes and keep your focus on the drop.

Compare access

Free

  • Play Classic, Endless, Daily Drop and Catch.
  • Spend 500 coins for one run of an eligible Premium challenge mode.
  • Optional rewarded ads may offer coins or an eligible survival continue.
  • Forced interstitial ads may appear after eligible runs.

Premium

  • Play Precision, Extreme, Micro and Micro Catch while your subscription is active.
  • Forced interstitial ads are suppressed.
  • Optional rewarded ads remain available.

Monthly or yearly

Choose an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription in Perfect Drop. Apple processes payment through the App Store. The current localized price and plan are shown in the app before you subscribe.

Purchase, restore or manage

Open Settings in Perfect Drop to purchase or restore an eligible Premium subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple's subscription settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal; cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current paid period. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its policies.

Ads

Premium suppresses forced interstitial ads; it does not remove optional rewarded ads. Rewarded placements depend on availability and remain your choice.
