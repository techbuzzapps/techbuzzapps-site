Perfect Drop Support

Quick answers to help you get back to the next drop.

Getting started and game modes

How do I play Perfect Drop?

Time your release so the ball lands on the target. A closer landing earns a better result and helps build a Perfect streak.

What are the game modes?

Classic: a 60-second timing run. Endless: keep dropping as the challenge progresses. Daily Drop: the current day's fixed 10-drop challenge with three lives. Catch: drag the target to catch automatically released balls. Precision: survive as the target moves and shrinks. Extreme: a faster, smaller-target survival challenge. Micro: survive with a tiny target. Micro Catch: drag to catch the ball on a tiny target. Precision, Extreme, Micro and Micro Catch are Premium modes; eligible modes also offer a 500-coin one-run pass.

Daily Drop, scoring and Perfect streaks

How does Daily Drop work?

Daily Drop uses a fixed 10-drop sequence for your current day. You have three lives. You can replay the sequence and improve your score; completing all ten drops counts toward the daily completion and streak once that day.

How are drops scored?

Each landing is graded PERFECT, GREAT, GOOD or MISS. A PERFECT streak counts consecutive PERFECT landings; a different result breaks the streak.

Coins, rewards and progression

How can I earn coins?

A Classic run finished without a MISS awards 25 coins. Completing Daily Drop awards 25 coins once per day. An optional rewarded coin ad awards 10 coins, up to three times per day, when the reward is confirmed. Earn XP from play, Perfect landings, Daily Drop and milestones to progress through levels and unlock collectible balls.

What can I spend coins on?

Spend 500 coins for one run of an eligible Premium challenge mode. In an eligible survival run, one continue costs 100 coins; an available rewarded ad can also offer a continue. Coin passes unlock one run only and do not include Premium benefits.

Game Center leaderboards and achievements

What can I see in Game Center?

Perfect Drop supports Classic and Endless best-score boards, a Daily Drop score board, a shared Longest Perfect Streak board, and 11 achievements. Game Center requires an Apple account signed in to Game Center and Apple's service availability.

Why did a score or achievement not appear?

Check that Game Center is signed in and your device has a connection. The app keeps local progress and can submit eligible best scores and earned achievements when you sign in; submissions may take time or fail if Apple's service is unavailable. Open the Game Center dashboard again later to check.

What happens if I switch Game Center accounts?

Apple controls the active Game Center account. Local game progress stays on your device, and eligible local records may be submitted after you sign in. If you use a different account on the same device, check that the intended Game Center account is active before playing or syncing.

Premium and purchases

What does Premium include?

Monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions unlock Precision, Extreme, Micro and Micro Catch while active and suppress forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available. See Premium details.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Perfect Drop Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the Apple Account used to subscribe, then try again with a connection. Apple handles subscription billing and refunds.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Manage or cancel through Apple's subscription settings. Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of your current paid period.

Advertisements and continues

Why do I still see ads with Premium?

Premium suppresses forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available by choice, including ads that offer coins or a survival continue.

Why isn't a rewarded ad available?

Rewarded ads depend on consent, connection and ad availability, and may not always load. Coin rewards are added only after the ad confirms they were earned.

Troubleshooting

Premium is active but a challenge mode is locked. What should I try?

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases, confirm the Apple Account used for the subscription, and allow StoreKit a moment to refresh. If the mode remains locked, contact support with your device model and app version. Never send your password or full payment details.

How do I manage privacy or notification choices?

Where available, use Privacy Options in Perfect Drop Settings for Google's consent choices. Manage Apple's tracking permission and notification permission in iOS Settings.
