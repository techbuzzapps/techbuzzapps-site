Daily Drop

Today's challenge uses a fixed 10-drop sequence with three lives. Complete all ten drops to log the day's completion and keep your streak moving; you can replay the challenge to try for a better score.

Coins, Rewards and Progress

Earn coins, XP and progress toward levels, milestones and collectible balls as you play. A Classic run finished without a MISS awards 25 coins; completing Daily Drop awards 25 once per day. Optional rewarded coin ads award 10 coins, up to three times per day. Spend 500 coins for one run of an eligible Premium challenge mode, or 100 coins for one eligible survival continue per run.

Game Center

When Game Center is available and you are signed in, Perfect Drop can submit eligible Classic and Endless best scores, Daily Drop scores, your longest Perfect streak and earned achievements. Apple manages your Game Center account; score and achievement availability depends on Apple's services and account configuration.

Perfect Drop Premium

Monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions unlock Precision, Extreme, Micro and Micro Catch while active, and suppress forced interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available. Play an eligible Premium challenge once for 500 coins, or learn about Premium.

Compatibility

Requires iOS 18.6 or later.
