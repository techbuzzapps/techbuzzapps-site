Perfect Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Perfect Drop is a precision timing game independently developed and published by an individual developer under the TechBuzzApps brand. This policy describes how information is stored and handled when you use Perfect Drop on iOS.

2. Game Data and Progress

Perfect Drop stores game data on your device using iOS app storage. This includes scores and best records, Perfect streaks, coins, XP and levels, Daily Drop attempts and streaks, milestones and collectible ball progress, and preferences such as sound, haptics, onboarding and reminders. The app also stores local records of Game Center submissions and achievements waiting to sync.

Perfect Drop does not require you to create a separate Perfect Drop account to play. Game Center, Apple subscriptions and advertising services are separate services described below.

3. Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Perfect Drop can submit eligible Classic and Endless best scores, Daily Drop scores, Perfect streak records and earned achievements to Apple. The app keeps local records and may submit eligible progress when you sign in, including progress earned before sign-in. Apple manages your Game Center account and its records under Apple's Game Center privacy practices.

Game Center submissions require Apple's service and an authenticated account. The app's local progress remains on your device independently of Game Center availability.

4. Advertising and Consent

Perfect Drop uses Google Mobile Ads for interstitial and optional rewarded ads, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. UMP refreshes consent information when the app launches and presents any applicable privacy form. If Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission has not been answered, Perfect Drop can then show Apple's native ATT prompt. UMP choices and ATT permission are separate. Ad requests are made only when UMP allows them; declining ATT does not by itself prevent ads that UMP permits.

Google says its Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device identifiers including an advertising identifier, advertising data, app or ad interactions, diagnostics, crash logs and performance data. Google may use these categories for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention or service performance. The information involved can depend on the SDK, ad configuration, device settings, consent and ATT choices. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's UMP information and Google's Privacy Policy.

When available, you can revisit Google's consent choices through Privacy Options in Perfect Drop Settings. You can review or change Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings. An SDK's data disclosures do not mean every listed category is processed in every ad request.

5. Premium Subscriptions

Perfect Drop offers optional monthly and yearly Premium subscriptions through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes payment and subscription transactions. Perfect Drop uses verified subscription entitlement information to provide Premium access. For Apple's handling of App Store information, see Apple's App Store privacy information.

6. Notifications

Perfect Drop may schedule optional Daily Drop and comeback reminders as local notifications on your device. The app requests notification permission when you choose to enable reminders, not at launch. Notification content is scheduled locally; the reviewed implementation does not use remote push notifications. Manage notification permission in iOS Settings and reminder preferences in Perfect Drop.

7. Third-Party Services

Perfect Drop uses Apple Game Center, StoreKit and App Tracking Transparency, and Google Mobile Ads and UMP for the features explained above. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Perfect Drop's local gameplay storage is separate from information Apple or Google process through their services.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay, progression and preferences remain in local app storage until removed by the operating system or app data is otherwise reset; Perfect Drop sets no fixed retention period. The app does not provide an in-app tool to export, reset or delete all local progress. Local app data may be included in iOS backups and could return if a backup is restored; deleting the app does not necessarily remove a copy in a backup.

Information submitted to Game Center, StoreKit or advertising providers is handled under those providers' retention and deletion practices. Perfect Drop does not set a retention period for information held by those providers. For privacy questions, contact techbuzzapps@gmail.com.

9. Privacy Choices

You can manage Apple's ATT permission and notification access in iOS Settings; manage Game Center through your Apple account and device settings; manage subscriptions through Apple; and revisit advertising consent through Privacy Options in the app when UMP makes that option available. You can also contact us with privacy questions at techbuzzapps@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians with questions about a child's privacy while using Perfect Drop can contact techbuzzapps@gmail.com.

11. Security

Perfect Drop stores gameplay and settings using iOS app storage and relies on platform protections for app data. Apple and Google handle information sent through their services under their own security practices. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. International Services

Apple and Google process information through their services under their own privacy practices and applicable requirements. For current provider information, see Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

13. Changes and Contact Information

This policy may be updated when Perfect Drop, its services or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.

Perfect Drop is independently developed and published by an individual developer under the TechBuzzApps brand. For privacy questions, support or requests relating to your data, contact techbuzzapps@gmail.com or visit techbuzzapps.com.
